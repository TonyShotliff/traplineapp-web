Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use Trapline?
No. Trapline stores all your data locally on your device — no account or sign-in required.

How do I export my data?
Trapline Pro subscribers can export their full data from the app using the phone's native share sheet (email, Files, cloud storage, etc.).

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through your device's App Store or Google Play account settings, not inside the app itself.

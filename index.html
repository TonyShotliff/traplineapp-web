Why Trapline

Built for the real trapline, not the backyard

Most trapping apps try to manage you — a required target species, a check-status to fill in every single day. Trapline isn't a constant. It's a diary.

No overhandling, ever
Other apps force a target species per set and make you mark every trap checked or missed. Trapline just records what actually happened — nothing is ever required, not even on check day.
Your own inventory, not a generic list
Build your own bait, lure, urine, and trap type lists as you actually use them — not a locked dropdown built for every trapper's specialty but yours. Load it all in before the season starts, or just add new gear on the fly right at the set the first time you use it — either way works.
Quick-tap pickers, built for gloves
Your most-used gear sorts to the front automatically. No typing, no dropdown menus to fight with — just tap and go, even with cold, wet hands.
Real season analytics
The Reports tab turns a season of logging into answers — which bait/lure/urine combos actually produced, your catch rate, and a full breakdown by set.
FHA price comparison — nobody else has this
Log what you actually sold each species for and see it stacked against real Fur Harvesters Auction reference pricing. No other trapping app tracks this at all.
